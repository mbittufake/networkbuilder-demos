Questions, answered

Can you cut my logo's exact shape? −

Yes — send any PNG/AI. We create contour cutline around your artwork (text, mascot, illustration). Border or borderless, with 2mm bleed. Free proof shows cutline.

Which shape for jars vs boxes? +

Jars/bottles: circle or transparent no-label. Boxes/pouches: rectangle or square. Promo: star/hex/holo. Unsure? Use custom builder recommendation.

Any colour & pattern? +

Full CMYK + white ink. Solids, gradients, foil gold, kraft texture, holographic prism, clear. Pantone matching available.
