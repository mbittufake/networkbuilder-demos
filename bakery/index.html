Our Story

A small kitchen,
a big appetite for good.

Crumb & Craft began in 2014 in Maya's home oven — sourdough loaves for friends, gradually turning into a neighbourhood ritual. Eleven years later, we still mill our flour in-house, source butter from a co-op 60km away, and wake up at 4 AM to greet the dough.

We don't do shortcuts, preservatives, or "mass production." We do honest, slow craft — and we'd love to bake for you.
