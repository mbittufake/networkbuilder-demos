Our Beginning

Maya's first loaf, baked in 2014.

It started on a Saturday morning when Maya, a former hotel pastry chef, pulled her first sourdough boule out of a borrowed oven. Her apartment building queued up. The next week, the building next door. By month three, she was baking 40 loaves every Friday night.

"We didn't have a business plan. We had a recipe, a grinder, and a 4 AM alarm. The rest just kept happening — slowly, the way dough does."

In 2018, with husband Arjun joining full-time, Crumb & Craft moved into a real bakery on Rash Behari Road. Today, five bakers, two ovens, and one obsession later, we're still doing exactly the same thing: making honest, slow, hand-crafted bakes that taste like someone cared.

Our Promise

Four things we'll never compromise on.

🌾

Local grain, always

We mill our atta, maida and whole wheat fresh every week from a single farm in Punjab.

⏰

Slow is the recipe

Our sourdough ferments for 48 hours. Croissants laminate for 6. Good things need time.

👨‍🍳

Hand over machine

Every loaf is shaped, scored, and finished by a real human. Not a production line.

🌱

Nothing fake, ever

No preservatives, no shortcuts, no "bakery flavours." Real butter, real vanilla, real work.

A Day in the Kitchen

From flour to your hands.

Every item you eat has been touched by ~7 humans, over the span of 12–48 hours. Here's the journey.

3AM

Karan feeds the mother starter

Our 6-year-old wild yeast starter gets a mix of fresh flour and filtered water. By morning it'll have tripled.

🌾 Organic atta 💧 RO water
4AM

First dough goes in

Slow fermentation begins in the cool room. Sourdough ferments for 48 hours; brioche overnight.

6AM

Lamination + shaping

The pastry team layers butter into dough — 27 folds for a perfect croissant. Cakes get their final glaze.

7AM

Doors open. The bake begins.

The first loaves come out at golden-brown perfection. The kettle goes on. The smell goes everywhere.

by3PM

Sold out. Most days.

Anything left gets donated to the local children's home. Nothing sits overnight. Tomorrow, we start again.
