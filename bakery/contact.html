Common Questions

FAQ

Do you ship across India?
Currently we deliver within West Bengal and to NCR via overnight partner couriers. For the rest of India, we ship shelf-stable items like biscotti, cookies, and our signature brown butter shortbread. Email us for a custom shipping quote.
How far in advance should I order a custom cake?
Birthday & theme cakes need at least 48 hours. Wedding & multi-tier cakes need 2–4 weeks so we can do a design consult, mockups, and a tasting. Last-minute custom orders? Call us — sometimes we can squeeze you in.
Are your items eggless / vegan / gluten-free?
Yes! We have a dedicated eggless and vegan bakery line — most breads, croissants (with cultured vegan butter), and cakes have a plant-based alternative. For custom orders, tick the boxes in step 2 and we'll quote accordingly.
Where do you source your flour?
100% from Punjab — we work directly with two farms that grow heritage wheat for us. The atta is stone-milled fresh every week in our kitchen. Butter comes from a Sahiwal co-op 60km away.
Do you sell wholesale to cafés?
Yes — we currently supply 12 cafés and 4 hotels in Kolkata & Malda. Breads, viennoiserie, and biscuits are available for daily delivery. Email wholesale@crumbcraft.in for a wholesale deck & pricing.
