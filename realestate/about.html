Our Story

15 Years of Connecting
Families to Dream Homes

Estate was founded in 2009 with a simple belief: buying or renting a home should be a joyful, transparent, and stress-free experience for every Indian family.

Our Mission

Making Real Estate
Radically Simple

We started Estate because we personally experienced how painful, opaque, and exhausting property transactions could be. Hidden fees, unreliable agents, and unverified listings made what should be a life milestone into a nightmare.

Today, we operate across 18 cities, with a team of 200+ verified agents and 4,200+ personally-inspected properties. Every listing on Estate is real — no ghost listings, no fake photos, no hidden charges.
