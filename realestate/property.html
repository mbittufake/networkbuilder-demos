About This Property

Welcome to Villa Serenova — a masterpiece of modern architecture nestled in the heart of Juhu, Mumbai's most coveted residential enclave. This stunning 4 BHK independent villa has been meticulously designed to offer a seamless blend of contemporary aesthetics and timeless comfort.

Spread across 2,400 sq.ft of built-up area on a 3,600 sq.ft plot, Villa Serenova features soaring ceiling heights, Italian marble flooring, a chef's modular kitchen with imported fittings, and a private rooftop terrace with city views.

Located just 5 minutes from Juhu Beach and 10 minutes from Mumbai Airport, this property offers unmatched connectivity to western suburbs, BKC, and South Mumbai.
