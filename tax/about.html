✦ Our Story

From a 2-person Mumbai desk in 2009 to India's most-trusted CA firm.

TaxMitra started in 2009 with two Chartered Accountants, one rented desk at Nariman Point, and a simple belief: taxpayers deserve honest advice, not just a return. 17 years later, we're a 21-Chartered Accountant, 6-Specialist, ICAI-licensed firm headquartered in Mumbai & Delhi NCR with branches in Bengaluru & Ahmedabad.

We've filed 17,000+ ITRs, defended 350+ IT notices, incorporated 900+ companies, and saved Indian taxpayers cumulatively ₹35 Cr+ legally.

We are appointed advisors to leading Indian founders, HNIs, VC-funded SaaS startups, NRI family offices and listed corporations. Our work is trusted because we never file what we cannot defend in ITAT.
