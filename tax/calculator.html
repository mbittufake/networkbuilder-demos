✦ Calculator FAQs

Tax Regime Questions, Answered

Which regime is usually better?

For income under ₹15L with limited investments, New Regime wins. Above ₹15L with heavy 80C/80D/HRA/home loan, Old Regime usually wins. The real answer: it depends.

Can I switch regimes every year?

Yes, salaried employees can choose Old or New regime each financial year. Business / freelancer taxpayers can switch once in their lifetime (now allowed from FY 24-25).

What about capital gains?

Capital gains are computed separately under both regimes using Section 111A & 112A rates — savings from this calculator don't include capital gains. Ask our CAs for the full picture.

Is this calculation accurate?

Yes — slabs & cess match Finance Act 2025 / CBDT Notification 25/2025. For ₹1 Cr+ incomes, multi-source income or NRI cases please request a manual CA review.
