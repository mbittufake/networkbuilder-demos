✦ FAQs

Frequently asked about our CA services

Are all your CAs ICAI-licensed? Can I verify the firm registration?

Yes — every senior partner is a Fellow Chartered Accountant (FCA) with active ICAI membership. You can verify our firm at ICAI Firm Reg #054921 on the ICAI portal. We share the partner DIN and membership number on every engagement letter.

What's the typical turnaround time for an ITR-2 capital gains filing?

Once we receive your Form 16, broker P&L, AIS, and bank statements (typically via WhatsApp), a Senior CA computes, signs and files your ITR-2 within 3 working days. E-verification is supported remotely via Aadhaar OTP.

Do you handle Income Tax notice replies & ITAT appeals?

Yes. We have a dedicated Tax Litigation desk led by Adv. Vikramaditya Roy (Senior Counsel). We handle u/s 143(1), 148, 154, 263 and search & seizure cases. Our appellate success rate at CIT(A) is 91% and at ITAT is 94%.

What documents do I share? Is my data secure?

You can share PDFs via WhatsApp, a secure client portal, or in-person dropbox. All client data is stored on India-hosted, AES-256 encrypted servers. We are bound by ICAI confidentiality rules and sign NDAs on request.

Do you offer NRI and foreign-asset tax services?

Absolutely. We file ITRs with Schedule FSI, TR, FA, AL for NRIs / OCIs / RNORs holding US stocks, ESOPs, ESOP RSU, crypto, NRO/NRE bank accounts. We also handle DTAA residency, Form 10F, TRC, and FEMA remittance compliance.
