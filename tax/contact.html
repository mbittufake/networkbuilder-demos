✦ FAQs

Common contact questions

Is the first consultation truly free?

Yes — your first 15-minute CA call is unconditionally free, with zero obligation to engage. We will discuss your concern, scope, and recommended next steps.

How quickly will a Senior CA respond?

WhatsApp messages are replied to within 2 working hours. Form submissions receive a call-back on the same business day. For IT notices (Scrutiny / u/s 148), we activate our 24×7 crisis hotline.

I'm outside Mumbai / Delhi / Bengaluru / Ahmedabad — can you still help?

Absolutely. 50%+ of our engagements are remote-document, fully digital. WhatsApp us your Form 16 / bank statements / Form 26AS and our CA team will handle your entire case file remotely. We serve clients in 200+ Indian cities and 12 countries.

Is my data confidential?

Yes. We are bound by ICAI's professional confidentiality code. All data is stored on India-hosted, AES-256 encrypted servers. We sign NDAs on request and never share your documents with any third party.

Which language can I communicate in?

English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and Tamil. For other languages, we'll arrange an interpreter within 24 hours of your request.
